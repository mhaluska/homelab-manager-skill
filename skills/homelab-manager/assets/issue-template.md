### Summary
One or two sentences: what is wrong.

### Affected
Hosts, VMIDs, groups, files or components (e.g. `dns01 (VMID 100)`, `tofu/environments/homelab/homelab.auto.tfvars`).

### Evidence
The command you ran, when, and its trimmed output. Data only, no secrets.

### Impact
What breaks or is at risk if this stays, and how soon.

### Proposed fix
The change you'd make (files, commands), and whether it needs approval (deletion, reboot, storage, cluster).

### Verify
Command(s) whose output shows the problem is gone.
